## whoami

I'm a developer focused on the Android open-source ecosystem, especially **Motorola devices and the Moto G82 5G (rhodep)**.

My work spans device bring-up, custom ROM integration, kernel and vendor trees, and custom recovery development. I like understanding how things work under the hood — and making them work on devices that deserve more than their stock software.
